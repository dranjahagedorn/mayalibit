Villages

Go
Southern Entrance

A lively village at the bay's southern entrance – snorkel crystal-clear waters, kayak by traditional canoe, spot dolphins, explore waterfalls and mangroves, and visit local weavers onshore.

Desa Go di pintu masuk selatan teluk menawarkan snorkeling, kayak dengan perahu tradisional, pengamatan lumba-lumba, air terjun, dan jelajah mangrove – serta kunjungan ke pengrajin anyaman lokal.

Waifoi
Cultural Heart

Waifoi is the cultural heart of Mayalibit Bay – home to a Ma'ya ancestral house, traditional sago processing, and the Makai Hun Hat cultural stage. The surrounding forest and mangroves shelter rich wildlife, from amphibians to endemic birds.

Waifoi adalah jantung budaya Teluk Mayalibit – dengan rumah adat Ma'ya, pengolahan sagu tradisional, dan sanggar budaya Makai Hun Hat. Hutan dan mangrove di sekitarnya menjadi habitat satwa liar yang kaya.

Warimak
Into the Wild

Remote and unhurried, Warimak rewards adventurous visitors with jungle trekking, cave exploration, and exceptional birdwatching amid untouched forest rich in flora and fauna.

Terpencil dan tenang, Warimak menawarkan trekking hutan, jelajah goa, dan pengamatan burung yang luar biasa di tengah hutan yang kaya flora dan fauna.

Kalitoko
River & Forest

Kalitoko sits deep in the bay, where rivers meet mangroves and the forest begins. Ideal for slow travel – trekking, river exploration, and quiet encounters with wildlife.

Kalitoko terletak jauh di dalam teluk, di mana sungai bertemu mangrove dan hutan dimulai. Cocok untuk perjalanan santai – trekking, jelajah sungai, dan pengamatan satwa.

Warsambin
Blue Waters & Dance

Warsambin welcomes visitors with traditional dance and the striking blue waters of Kalibiru. The village is also a gateway to mangrove jetty exploration along the bay's eastern shore.

Warsambin menyambut tamu dengan tarian tradisional dan air biru Kalibiru yang memukau. Desa ini juga menjadi pintu masuk untuk menjelajahi jeti mangrove di pesisir timur teluk.

Mumes
Northern Seclusion

Mumes lies at the northern tip of the bay, offering a quiet base for those seeking seclusion – with glamping options and homestays amid pristine nature.

Mumes terletak di ujung utara teluk, menawarkan ketenangan bagi mereka yang mencari kedamaian – dengan pilihan glamping dan homestay di alam yang masih asri.
