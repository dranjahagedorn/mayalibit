4. Language preference

The website stores the language you select under the key “lang” in your browser’s local storage. This allows your preferred language to remain active when you move between pages and on a later visit.

The preference remains on your device until you choose another language or delete the website data in your browser. It is not sent to us or combined by us with other information.

The storage is necessary to provide the language version expressly requested by you. The legal basis under German law is Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG and, where personal data is involved, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The website currently uses Google Fonts to display the typefaces “Cormorant Garamond” and “Inter”. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google LLC in the United States may also be involved in providing the service.

When a page is opened, the browser connects to Google servers to retrieve font files and formatting information. The IP address, browser and operating-system information, the requested address and technical request data may be transmitted to Google.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a consistent, accessible and readable presentation. A transfer to the United States cannot be excluded. Further information is available in Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. External software libraries

When these files are retrieved, the IP address, time of access, requested file, browser information and referring page may be processed by the providers involved.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the reliable and user-friendly technical presentation of the website. Processing outside the European Economic Area may occur.
