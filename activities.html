Activities

Experience Mayalibit Bay at the pace of its communities — on the water, beneath the rainforest canopy and through the living traditions of the villages.

Water & mangroves

Snorkel, kayak or travel by local boat through clear water, river mouths and quiet mangrove channels. Depending on the village, experiences can also include fishing and dolphin watching.

Rainforest & wildlife

Follow village guides on forest walks, longer treks and cave visits. Birdwatching and quiet wildlife encounters reveal another side of Raja Ampat beyond its reefs.

Culture & community

Learn about weaving, traditional sago processing, dance and everyday village life. A local homestay makes it possible to spend more time with the people who know the bay best.
